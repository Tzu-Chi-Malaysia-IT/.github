# Company Profile
## Tzu Chi Kuala Lumpur & Selangor Branch (Malaysia)
### Organization Overview

Tzu Chi Kuala Lumpur & Selangor Branch is the central regional branch of the Taiwan Buddhist Tzu Chi Foundation Malaysia, a global humanitarian and charitable organization founded by Dharma Master Cheng Yen in Taiwan in 1966. The branch serves as a major hub for Tzu Chi's charitable, medical, educational, environmental, and community development activities throughout Kuala Lumpur and Selangor.

### History

The global Tzu Chi Foundation was established in Taiwan in 1966 by Dharma Master Cheng Yen with a mission to relieve suffering and promote compassion through humanitarian service. The Kuala Lumpur and Selangor operations began in the mid-1990s and were officially elevated to branch status in February 2006, becoming one of the key centers of Tzu Chi's activities in Malaysia.

### Mission

To serve humanity through compassion, selfless giving, and community service while promoting moral values, environmental sustainability, education, and healthcare for all members of society regardless of race, religion, or background.

### Core Areas of Service
1. Charity & Humanitarian Aid
    - Financial assistance for underprivileged families
    - Food aid and essential supplies distribution
    - Support for elderly persons, single-parent families, and vulnerable communities
    - Community outreach and home visitation programs
2. Medical Services
    - Free medical clinics
    - Healthcare support programs
    - Medical assistance for needy patients
    - Health awareness and screening activities
3. Education
    - Educational assistance and scholarships
    - Continuing education programs
    - Character-building and values education
    - Early childhood education through Tzu Chi kindergartens
4. Environmental Protection
    - Recycling and environmental awareness campaigns
    - Community sustainability projects
    - Public education on waste reduction and responsible living
5. Disaster Relief
    - Emergency response and relief operations
    - Distribution of food, supplies, and necessities during disasters
    - Long-term recovery and rebuilding support for affected communities

## Facilities

The Kuala Lumpur Tzu-Chi Jing Si Hall serves as the branch headquarters and community center. It functions as a venue for volunteer training, educational activities, charitable services, cultural programs, and community events. The facility is conveniently located near the MRT Putrajaya Line's Kepong Baru Station.

## Community Impact

Through a large network of volunteers, Tzu Chi Kuala Lumpur & Selangor Branch has contributed significantly to community welfare initiatives, healthcare support, educational development, environmental conservation, and disaster relief efforts across Malaysia. The organization continues to foster a culture of kindness and civic responsibility through volunteer-led programs and community engagement.

## Corporate Summary

Tzu Chi Kuala Lumpur & Selangor Branch is one of Malaysia's leading humanitarian non-profit organizations. As part of the global Tzu Chi Foundation, it works to improve lives through charity, healthcare, education, environmental protection, and disaster relief while promoting compassion and social harmony throughout the community.
